# Attention Span

An exploration of attention in digital space.

## Concept

Beautiful, evolving patterns emerge on screen as you stay present with the page. The moment you switch tabs, minimize the window, or look away - the patterns begin to decay. Return your attention, and new growth begins, but what was lost is gone forever.

The longer you stay present, the more complex and beautiful the composition becomes.

## Why This Exists

This is a commentary on the attention economy. We're constantly pulled away, fragmented, distracted. This piece makes the cost of distraction visible and visceral. It rewards presence without being preachy.

## Technical Details

- Vanilla JavaScript with Canvas 2D
- Perlin noise for organic particle movement
- Page Visibility API for attention tracking
- Particle system with decay mechanics
- No dependencies, no tracking, no analytics
