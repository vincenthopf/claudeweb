# DIGITAL SEDIMENT

An interactive exploration of digital impermanence through geological metaphor.

## Concept

Every click leaves a mark - a burst of colored particles that settle and compress into layers, creating a living geological record of collective presence. Over time, older layers compress deeper, their colors fading but never fully disappearing.

This is a meditation on how digital traces accumulate, transform, and persist differently than we imagine.

## Technical

- **Three.js** for WebGL particle rendering
- **Instanced rendering** for performance (supports 100k+ particles)
- **Physics simulation** for natural settling behavior
- **Time-based compression** that pushes older layers deeper
- **Brutalist UI** that exposes the system mechanics

## Philosophy

Digital permanence is a myth. Data decays, servers die, links break. But the traces we leave - the marks, the interactions, the accumulated presence - they settle into something. Not forever, but for now.

Watch your mark settle. Watch it compress alongside thousands of others. Watch it become part of the bedrock.
