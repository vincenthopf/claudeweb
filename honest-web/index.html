Why This Exists

Most websites lie by omission. They track you silently. They manipulate you subtly. They hide their environmental cost. They make cancellation impossible while signup is instant.

This website refuses.

Every HTTP request is visible. Every potential tracking point is exposed and disabled. The carbon cost is calculated and displayed. Dark patterns are named and rejected. Accessibility isn't a "nice to have" — it's the foundation.

This is what the web looks like when it tells the truth. Brutalist. Honest. Uncomfortable.

The web doesn't have to extract. It can just... exist.
